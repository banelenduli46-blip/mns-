About MNS

Practical support for project delivery

MNS Holdings & Investments is a South African-owned company serving government, parastatal and private-sector clients. Our work spans goods supply, logistics, cleaning, general and professional support, construction, refurbishment and property opportunities.

We begin by understanding the client's requirements, timeline and procurement needs. Depending on the project, we deliver directly or coordinate suitable suppliers and partners, with responsibilities and scope agreed before work begins.

What we do

Services aligned to real delivery needs

Supply of goods and materials

We source and coordinate goods and materials against your technical specifications, quantities, budget parameters and required delivery dates. We clarify product details with suppliers, coordinate pricing and delivery arrangements, and keep the supply plan aligned with the project brief so expectations are clear before an order proceeds.

General services

We provide facilities, site and day-to-day operational support shaped around your organisation's requirements. The scope can be defined around the tasks needed, service frequency, site access and operating hours, with responsibilities and communication arrangements agreed before work begins.

Subcontracting & joint ventures

Where an opportunity calls for additional capacity or specialist capability, we can coordinate with suitable contractors and suppliers through subcontracting or joint-venture arrangements. The proposed roles, work packages, reporting lines and delivery responsibilities are discussed in relation to the tender requirements and agreed project scope.

Professional support services

Our professional support can include project coordination, administrative assistance, procurement support and tender-document preparation. We help organise requirements, supporting information, submission schedules and action items, with the exact tasks agreed to suit the client's process and the scope of the engagement.

Construction & refurbishment

We coordinate building repairs, upgrades and refurbishment for commercial or public-facing spaces, based on the agreed specifications and site requirements. Planning may cover work sequencing, access, materials, trades and key milestones, with progress coordinated against the agreed scope and schedule.

Property & investment

We consider property acquisition, development and investment opportunities on a case-by-case basis. Initial assessments can consider location, intended use, condition, development requirements and commercial considerations; each opportunity is subject to further review, and investment outcomes are not guaranteed.

Logistics services

We coordinate transport, warehousing and distribution to support the movement of goods between suppliers, storage locations and project sites. Depending on the brief, arrangements may include collection and delivery schedules, storage needs, delivery coordination and status communication aligned with project timelines.

Cleaning services

We arrange cleaning for offices, shared facilities and public-facing spaces according to the facility's needs and operating schedule. Service plans can define the areas to be covered, agreed task lists, service frequency, access arrangements and any site-specific requirements.

Working with MNS

A clear approach from enquiry to delivery

Understand the requirement

We start by understanding the service required, project location, scope, quantities, timeframe and procurement route. For tender-related enquiries, sharing the reference number, submission deadline and relevant specifications helps us understand the opportunity.

Agree the scope

We discuss the proposed deliverables, responsibilities, site or supply requirements, and any suppliers or delivery partners involved. The scope, assumptions, key dates and practical arrangements are clarified so both sides understand what is included before commitments are made.

Coordinate delivery

Once the engagement is agreed, we coordinate the activities, suppliers and schedules required for delivery. We provide relevant progress updates, raise timing or scope issues that need attention, and coordinate next steps in line with the agreed responsibilities.

Review and follow through

We confirm outstanding requirements, delivery records or handover items relevant to the engagement. Any follow-up actions are discussed with the client and handled according to the agreed scope.
