Practical support for project delivery

Four partners, equal shareholders, one standard: bids that are accurate and commitments that are kept.

MNS identifies, bids for and delivers on opportunities with disciplined execution and accountable governance. We work directly, or coordinate suitable suppliers and partners where extra capacity or specialist capability is needed, with scope agreed before work begins.

We are at start-up stage. Our priority is building a solid compliance foundation and a verifiable track record, then growing capacity in step with proven delivery.

Vision

A trusted, multi-provincial name in tender-based service delivery, known for reliability and compliance integrity.

Mission

Frequently asked questions

How do I request a quotation?

Select Get in Touch and tell us the service, location, scope or quantities and your timeline. For tenders, include the reference number and closing date.

Can my business partner or subcontract with MNS?

Yes. Send us your company details, services and areas of operation. Expressions of interest do not guarantee appointment or inclusion on any supplier list.

Where do you operate?

From Durban, Pretoria and Johannesburg, serving KwaZulu-Natal, Gauteng and, over time, nationally.

Are you a registered government supplier?

Our company is registered with CIPC (2026/776364/07); tax compliance, B-BBEE and POPIA compliance are in place, and CSD registration is in progress. We will publish verified details here once they are issued.

How is my information used?

Only to respond to your enquiry. A full POPIA privacy notice is being finalised with legal review.
